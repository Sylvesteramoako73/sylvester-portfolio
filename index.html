What I took from it

Three lessons for marketing property in Ghana

Send each click where it was promised

A banner for one estate lands on that estate's page, never the homepage. That alone kept visitors engaged for over a minute on average.

Mix reach with intent

News site ads build reach fast. Search and AI assistants bring people already looking. Relying on one channel leaves you paying for every visit.

Report the full picture

Totals can fall while the right pages rise. Clients get both numbers, and the plan follows the pages that are winning.
